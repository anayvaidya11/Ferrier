# Procurement Constraint — No Physical Hardware

**Status:** binding. Supersedes any prior amendment authorizing hardware spend.
**Date:** 1 August 2026

WyZen will not purchase physical hardware of any kind during this build. No cameras,
no sensors, no fabricated parts, no test rigs, no bench instrumentation.

**Budget covers software only** — compute, licenses, subscriptions, cloud GPU.

The deliverable is a demonstrated and simulated design for the YC Spring 2027
application. Buildability is proven through physics, simulation, CAD, and software,
per MASTER_CONTEXT §2.1. Nothing in this program requires tangible purchase.

Any task that assumes physical hardware is out of scope and must be re-planned as a
simulated or derived artifact, or dropped. Any claim that would require a physical
measurement to substantiate must be labeled as unmeasured, per §4.3.
